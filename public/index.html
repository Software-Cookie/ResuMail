    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is ResuMail free?

    Yes. The Free plan includes 10 emails every month, resume analysis, the recommended template and your own templates. Paid packs add more emails, ready-made templates, manual entry and shorter gaps.

    Do I have to share my Gmail password?

    No. You create a Google App Password, which only lets ResuMail send email through Gmail. It is used to send and is never stored on our server.

    Does ResuMail use AI?

    No. Your resume is analysed with transparent rules inside your browser. It only leaves your device as the attachment on the emails you send.

    How many emails can I send in a day?

    Gmail allows about 500 emails a day. We recommend 50 to 100 a day. Your plan sets the minimum gap between emails: 30 seconds on Free, 10 on Trial, 5 on Standard and 2 on Premium.

    How do I pay for a plan?

    Pay once with any UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm, then enter the 12-digit UPI transaction ID. Your plan is activated after the payment is verified, usually within a few hours. There is no subscription.

    Do email packs expire?

    No. Trial and Standard emails stay in your account until you use them. Premium is a one-time payment for lifetime, unlimited sending.

    Will recruiters see the other people I emailed?

    No. Every recruiter receives a separate email addressed only to them, greeting them by name and mentioning their company.
