    How it works

    Built for job seekers, written for recruiters

    Every email is short, personal and easy for HR to scan, with your resume attached.

    Understands your resume

    Detects your skills, experience, education and best-fit roles. Everything stays editable.

    Writes a recruiter-ready pitch

    A clear introduction plus a quick snapshot of experience, skills, education and notice period.

    Personal for every HR

    Greets each recruiter by name and mentions their company. One separate email per person.

    Safe, steady sending

    Gaps between emails, auto-retry, pause/stop and a report, so your account stays healthy.
