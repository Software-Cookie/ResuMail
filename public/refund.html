Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

All plans are one-time payments. There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

When you can get a refund

  • Within 7 days of payment, if you have sent 10 or fewer emails with the plan you bought.
  • Duplicate payments for the same order are refunded in full.
  • Payments we could not verify: if your plan was not activated, send us your order number and a screenshot of the payment. We will activate the plan or refund you.

When refunds are not possible

  • After 7 days, or after more than 10 emails were sent with the plan.
  • Accounts suspended for spam or misuse.
  • Plans activated with a coupon.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original UPI account, card or bank account within 5–7 business days.
